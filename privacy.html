Privacy Policy for Candy Ultra

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

1. Overview

Candy Ultra ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. Candy Ultra is designed as a local-first, offline Match-3 puzzle game hosted at https://candycrusherultra.pages.dev/.

2. Data Collection and Retention

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII). All game progress, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your device using web storage technologies such as localStorage.

3. Third-Party Services

This web application operates without third-party analytics or ad-tracking networks. No personal data is shared with external vendors.

4. Security Protocols

Candy Ultra is strictly served over secure HTTPS connections, ensuring that communication between your browser and our hosting server (Cloudflare Pages) is fully encrypted.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact developer support at support@candycrusherultra.pages.dev.

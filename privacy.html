Official Policy

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of Candy Crush Ultra ("we", "our", or "the App") regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you access or play our game across web, mobile, Progressive Web App (PWA), or Android (APK / AAB) packaged builds.

Candy Crush Ultra was engineered from the ground up as an ad-free, privacy-preserving, local-first Match-3 puzzle game. We firmly believe in protecting player privacy and do not monetize through behavioral tracking, ads, or data resale.

✓ Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). No registration or account creation is required to play all 199 levels.

3. Data Collection & Advertising

Zero Third-Party Advertising: Candy Crush Ultra contains zero third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, IronSource, or Meta Audience Network). As a result:

  • We do not record your Advertising ID (AAID or IDFA).
  • We do not track you across other applications or websites.
  • We do not profile your usage habits or demographics.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, GPS location, or contacts.

4. Local Storage & Data Retention Disclosure

To deliver a seamless offline experience where you can pause and resume your puzzle journey at any time, the App saves game progress solely within your device's browser sandbox using standard client-side technologies:

  • HTML5 LocalStorage / IndexedDB: Used exclusively to remember your current level (1 to 199), high scores, 3-star accomplishments, booster inventories (Lollipop Hammers, Color Bombs, Free Switches), and sound/haptic toggles.
  • Client-Side Only: This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or database.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: You retain complete ownership and control over this data. You can delete all game progress at any time by clearing website storage in your browser or clearing App Data via Android system settings.

5. Security Measures & Protocol Standards

We enforce modern, industry-standard cybersecurity measures to protect our users:

  • Strict HTTPS / TLS Encryption: All web requests and PWA resources are delivered exclusively over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) with HSTS enabled to prevent eavesdropping and man-in-the-middle attacks.
  • Service Worker Isolation: The offline Service Worker operates strictly within the domain's scope (/) and only caches static presentation assets (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons, audio) for instant offline playability.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Candy Crush Ultra is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because we do not collect personal data of any kind, we do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

This privacy policy conforms to Google Play Developer Program Policies, specifically the User Data policy and Families policy. When distributed as an Android application package (APK or AAB via Trusted Web Activity or WebView wrapper), the application declares zero sensitive permissions and zero background tracking services.
